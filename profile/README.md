Pine Computer gives your product a cloud computer with an agent built in. Your product hands it a job; the work crosses websites, files and business software; and the finished work comes back, ready to open.

Built for developers creating assistants, back-office tools and products that do work for people.

### From a job to finished work

| Step | What happens |
| :--- | :--- |
| Create a computer | Give your product its own cloud computer through the SDK. |
| Hand it a job | Pine's agent works across the browser, files, documents and a shell. |
| Take the controls | Let your users watch the live screen, step in and hand control back. |
| Open the result | Bring the finished work back into your product. |

Pine's agent reads web pages as structure: what's on the page, what it can do and what changed. The live screen is there for people to follow the work and take over when needed.
